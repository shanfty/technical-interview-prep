# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock with Cooldown

You may buy and sell stock any number of times, but after selling your stock you must wait one day before buying again. Return the maximum profit. You may hold at most one share at a time.

Examples: `[1,2,3,0,2]` returns `3`; `[1]` returns `0`.

Source: [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/neetcode150)
