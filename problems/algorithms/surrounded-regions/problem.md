# Surrounded Regions

Given an `m x n` board of `'X'` and `'O'`, capture every region of `'O'` that is completely surrounded by `'X'` by changing it to `'X'`. An `'O'` connected to the boundary is not captured.

Example: `[[X,X,X,X],[X,O,O,X],[X,X,O,X],[X,O,X,X]]` becomes `[[X,X,X,X],[X,X,X,X],[X,X,X,X],[X,O,X,X]]`.

Source: [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/neetcode150)
