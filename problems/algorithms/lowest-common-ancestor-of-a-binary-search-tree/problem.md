# Lowest Common Ancestor of a Binary Search Tree

Given the root of a binary search tree and two distinct nodes `p` and `q`, return their lowest common ancestor. A node may be an ancestor of itself.

Example: in BST `[6,2,8,0,4,7,9,null,null,3,5]`, the LCA of `2` and `8` is `6`, and the LCA of `2` and `4` is `2`.

Source: [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/neetcode150)
