# Min Cost Climbing Stairs

Given a cost array where cost[i] is the cost of stepping on stair i, return the minimum cost to reach the top. You may start at step 0 or step 1 and may climb one or two steps at a time.

Source: https://neetcode.io/problems/min-cost-climbing-stairs

Implement `Solution.minCostClimbingStairs(int[] cost)`.
