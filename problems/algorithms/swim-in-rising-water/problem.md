# Swim in Rising Water

Given an `n x n` grid where `grid[r][c]` is the elevation of a cell, you start at `(0,0)` and want to reach `(n-1,n-1)`. At time `t`, you may enter cells with elevation at most `t`. Return the minimum time needed to reach the destination.

Examples: `[[0,2],[1,3]]` returns `3`; `[[0,1,2,3,4],[24,23,22,21,5],[12,13,14,15,16],[11,17,18,19,20],[10,9,8,7,6]]` returns `16`.

Source: [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/neetcode150)
