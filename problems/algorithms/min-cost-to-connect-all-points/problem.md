# Min Cost to Connect All Points

Given points on a 2-D plane, connect all points with the minimum total cost. The cost to connect two points is their Manhattan distance, and every point must be reachable from every other point through the chosen connections.

Example: points `[[0,0],[2,2],[3,10],[5,2],[7,0]]` have minimum cost `20`; two points `[[0,0],[1,1]]` have cost `2`.

Source: [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/neetcode150)
